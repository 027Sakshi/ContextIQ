# ContextIQ hackathon demo

## Recommended judge story — 3 to 5 minutes

### 1. One-click Google workspace

Open ContextIQ and choose **Continue with Google**. Explain that the same verified identity owns the ContextIQ workspace, Gmail data and Calendar actions; tokens are isolated per user and never committed to the repository.

### 2. Command Center

Show the business attention queue, open commitments and pending human-approved actions. Generate the **AI Morning Brief** and emphasize that the answer cites retrieved business evidence.

### 3. Intelligent Inbox

Open a high-impact renewal/demo/customer email. Show:

- intent and priority,
- sender trust / threat signals,
- consequence score,
- CRM/deal/account context,
- Context Graph,
- related history from semantic RAG,
- detected commitments.

### 4. Ask ContextIQ

Ask a decision question such as:

> Which customer or revenue item needs my attention first, and why?

Open the Evidence expanders. Point out that ContextIQ combines local semantic retrieval with exact keyword/name matching and instructs the LLM to answer only from retrieved evidence.

### 5. Human-approved action

Generate an AI reply. Edit it if desired, then click **Approve & Create Gmail Draft**. Open Gmail and show the saved draft — nothing was sent automatically.

For a demo/meeting email, approve and execute the suggested action and show the newly created Google Calendar event.

### 6. Close the loop

Return to the Command Center and show that ContextIQ is not an email summarizer; it creates a traceable loop from business context → evidence → reasoning → approval → real action.

## Judge-ready positioning

**ContextIQ is an AI decision and action layer over business communication.**

The product is intentionally built with a free-first stack and uses local retrieval/embeddings for business memory. External generative AI is optional; core ingestion, retrieval, scoring, evidence and safe-action controls continue to function without it.
